## 0.1.75
- Quitting the game is faster: Galaxies no longer takes its patches off while the game is closing.
- Lily pads and other floating plants sit on the water you see (from 0.1.70).
- Swimming, the camera and the underwater fog agree on where the water surface is, so you no longer go "underwater" above the drawn surface (from 0.1.71).

## 0.1.69
- Creature diving has moved to FiresMonsterOverhaul: Galaxies no longer makes creatures dive, and the "CreatureDivingEnabled", "CreatureMaxDiveDepth" and "CreatureDiveSpeed" settings are now read by FiresMonsterOverhaul (with Advanced Player Movement installed). (diving)
- With Advanced Player Movement installed, it handles the camera when your head goes underwater; Galaxies still handles the camera on rolling ship swells. Player diving is unchanged. (camera)

## 0.1.65
- Tides are held still under ice.
- Faster water current checks on every character.
- On ring worlds tides, currents and whirlpools stand down; normal worlds are unchanged.

## 0.1.61
- Bug fixes and improvements.

## 0.1.59
- Added blood moons: about one full moon in ten turns red and brings bigger tides (you can change how often, or turn it off).
- River tide settings now come from the server, so every player gets the same rivers.

## 0.1.58
- Removed an unused water setting (water looks now live in Fires Tossin Shade).
- The admin "ignore currents" choice now stays on instead of resetting each game start.
- Fixed the in-game help: settings are under F1 > Config settings, not F8.
- Water features keep working after you reconnect.

## 0.1.55
- Rivers, whirlpools and tides reset properly when you travel between worlds.
- Destroyed orbiting bodies come back.
- Lighter on dedicated servers.
