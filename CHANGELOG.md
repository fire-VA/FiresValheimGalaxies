## 0.1.75
- Quitting the game is faster: Galaxies no longer takes its patches off while the game is closing.

## 0.1.74
- Faster loading: with Fires Admin Terrain installed, Galaxies leaves the custom ice restore to it (it was done twice), and without it the check is much cheaper.

## 0.1.73
- Without Fires Admin Terrain installed, Galaxies no longer logs errors at load about missing terrain, water, land ice and land snow anchors. Those are Fires Admin Terrain features; Galaxies now leaves them off and says so in one info line.
- Fixed: the faster loading of terrain tiles when you come near them works again (it had switched itself off after a game update).

## 0.1.59

- Blood moons: about one full moon in ten (set with BloodMoonOneIn) is a blood moon.

- Water features keep working after you reconnect.
